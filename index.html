The Foundation

What is Web3?

Web3 represents a vision of a more decentralized internet where blockchain networks can support digital assets, decentralized applications and user-controlled interactions.


Blockchain technology provides the foundation by allowing information and transactions to be recorded across a distributed network rather than relying entirely on one central authority.

WEB3
Core Ideas

The Building Blocks of Web3

Three ideas help explain why blockchain technology is different from traditional centralized systems.

01 / DECENTRALIZATION

Distributed Control

Blockchain networks distribute records and verification across multiple participants instead of depending entirely on one central authority.

02 / OWNERSHIP

Digital Ownership

Cryptographic keys allow users to control access to blockchain accounts and digital assets.

03 / TRANSPARENCY

Verifiable Records

Blockchain records can be verified by network participants and linked using cryptographic hashes.
